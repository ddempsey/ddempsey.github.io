# David Dempsey: research papers

Professor at the University of Canterbury. I build physics-based and machine-learning models of the subsurface: geothermal and magmatic systems, induced seismicity, volcanic eruption forecasting, CO2 and hydrogen storage, and groundwater.

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, University of Canterbury, Christchurch, New Zealand. ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2135-5129

Each paper below links to a summary page. That page has a Markdown version (`index.md`), a BibTeX record and, where the publisher permits, an open PDF and the full text. A machine-readable catalogue is at https://ddempsey.github.io/papers.json and an agent guide at https://ddempsey.github.io/llms.txt.
